Prologue

A leading heavy equipment rental company, bringing comprehensive, quality equipment and punctuality to any crane job, anytime, anywhere in the UAE.

Friendship Hall — FHE — has been lifting in the United Arab Emirates since 1986: 40 years of real-world experience. One of the largest fleets of cranes, extensive lifting and rigging support, and trained operators and lifting supervisors, on call around the clock.
